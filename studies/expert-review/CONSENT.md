# Expert review consent record

Before collection, the coordinator records the following for each reviewer outside the public repository:

- study purpose and synthetic/advisory scope were explained;
- participation is voluntary and may stop at any time;
- no employer-confidential, customer, infrastructure-security, or personal data is requested;
- responses may be analyzed and reported in aggregate;
- name, employer, and quotations will not be published without separate explicit permission;
- the reviewer had an opportunity to ask questions; and
- consent date, protocol version `1.0`, pseudonymous reviewer ID, and consent confirmation.

This is a minimal project consent record, not an institutional review determination. If the work is conducted through a university, employer, publication, or funded study, obtain the applicable ethics or organizational review before recruiting participants.

# Water Investigation Agent: expert review brief

## The problem

When pressure, flow, and water-quality signals disagree, several explanations can remain plausible. Investigators must decide which observation to collect next while considering information value, cost, and delay.

## What the agent does

The Water Investigation Agent maintains competing explanations—contamination, leak, and sensor fault—and recommends one of four next observations. Its recommendation maximizes expected uncertainty reduction per relative cost. It is advisory software evaluated on synthetic Net3 and L-Town simulations; it has no control-system access.

## What we are asking

Review 30 short synthetic cases. For each case, first select the next observation you would request. Then reveal the agent's choice and rate whether it is reasonable and safe as advisory guidance. The exercise is expected to take 25–40 minutes and may be paused.

We are evaluating recommendation plausibility and clarity, not testing the reviewer. We will not request utility data, customer information, infrastructure details, or employer-confidential procedures. Results will be reported in aggregate unless you separately approve attribution.

## Current evidence and limits

The agent has a prespecified, reproducible benchmark across five independently seeded synthetic ensembles. Expert review adds domain grounding, but neither source establishes field performance. A utility-approved offline replay would still be required before any operational claim.

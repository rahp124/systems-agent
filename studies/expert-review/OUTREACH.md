# Expert recruitment plan

## Target list

Build a list of 12–20 people to obtain 3–5 completed reviews. Prioritize, in order:

1. drinking-water utility distribution, water-quality, or operations professionals;
2. university researchers working on EPANET/WNTR, distribution hydraulics, contamination response, or utility decision support;
3. consultants with direct water-distribution investigation experience; and
4. retired utility professionals who can review without employer-data concerns.

Record only public contact details and these tracking fields: contact, role/category, source, date contacted, response, eligibility, follow-up date, and final disposition. Do not place private contact data in the public repository.

## Initial message

**Subject:** 30-minute expert review of a water-investigation decision tool

Hello [Name],

I built a research prototype that recommends the next observation to collect when water-distribution signals could indicate contamination, a leak, or a sensor fault. It is advisory-only and currently evaluated on synthetic Net3 and L-Town scenarios.

I am looking for water-domain professionals to independently review 30 short cases. You would first choose the next observation yourself, then see and rate the agent's recommendation. The review takes approximately 25–40 minutes, requests no utility or customer data, and will be reported in aggregate unless you separately approve attribution.

Would you be willing to participate, or point me to someone whose work includes distribution operations, water quality, hydraulic modeling, or incident investigation? I can send a one-page brief and the review link before you decide.

Thank you,

[Name] · [affiliation] · [Live demo](https://rahp124.github.io/systems-agent/) · [Source](https://github.com/rahp124/systems-agent)

## Follow-up

Send one follow-up 5–7 business days later, then close the contact unless they respond:

Hello [Name]—I am following up once on the synthetic water-investigation review below. The study is a 25–40 minute advisory-recommendation assessment and does not request operational data. If it is outside your area, no response is needed. Thank you.

## Scheduling and handling

- Send the brief before the review link and answer scope questions without coaching case choices.
- Use the pilot reviewer first; revise wording only before final collection.
- Assign pseudonymous IDs and store the identity mapping separately.
- Do not send the coordinator answer key.
- Thank participants after completion; do not offer authorship or endorsement in exchange for a favorable rating.
- Ask separately for permission before quoting or naming anyone.

# Reviewer guide

Thank you for reviewing this prototype. Please use your normal professional judgment; there is no expected answer.

For every case:

1. Read only the scenario, initial telemetry, possible explanations, and available observations.
2. Choose exactly one next observation and rate your confidence from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
3. Submit or record that phase-one choice before revealing the agent recommendation.
4. Rate the recommendation from 1 (not reasonable) to 5 (highly reasonable).
5. Mark its use as advisory guidance as `safe`, `uncertain`, or `unsafe` and briefly explain disagreements.

Treat relative costs as comparisons within this prototype, not dollar estimates, and turnaround steps as abstract decision cycles rather than clock time. Initial beliefs are model estimates from synthetic telemetry, not calibrated field probabilities. Do not infer unshown network details. Use the missing-information field when a real investigation would require context that the case omits. Stop and contact the coordinator if phase two is visible before you record phase one.

The four choices are: field chlorine grab, lab chlorine assay, portable pressure reading, or wait for telemetry. The tool does not recommend treatment, flushing, isolation, or other operational interventions.

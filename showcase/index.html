When water-system signals conflict, decide what to measure next.

The problem

When pressure, flow, and water-quality readings disagree, investigators cannot tell whether they are seeing contamination, a leak, or a faulty sensor. Choosing the wrong follow-up check wastes time and limited field resources.

How we solve it

The agent compares the possible causes, scores the available checks by how much uncertainty they could remove for their cost, and recommends what evidence to collect next.

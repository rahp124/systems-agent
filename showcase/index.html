Why this exists

An anomaly is not a diagnosis.

Similar telemetry patterns can come from contamination, a hydraulic leak, or a faulty sensor. Acting on the first plausible explanation wastes time and can create false confidence.

This agent treats investigation as sequential decision-making: update beliefs from evidence, quantify uncertainty, and make the next measurement choice explicit and reviewable.

Method

From uncertain signal to auditable next step.

01

Maintain hypotheses

Contamination, leak, and sensor fault remain explicit probability distributions.

02

Score observations

Field chlorine, lab confirmation, portable pressure, and waiting are compared by expected information and cost.

03

Update and record

Returned evidence updates the posterior; the complete recommendation path remains inspectable.

Safety boundary

Read-only by design.

There is no interface for pumps, valves, dosing, flushing, or any actuator. Real operational validation requires approved de-identified historian replay, locked outcomes, security review, and utility governance.
